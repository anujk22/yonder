# Yonder's market and the competition entry

**Product:** Ask someone already there. Yonder aims to answer time-sensitive questions about public places before someone makes the trip.

**Audience:** People deciding whether a trip is worth making: recreational players, shoppers, commuters and people planning everyday outings. Yonder is not a campus app. Student status describes the builders and enables the Next Gen submission category; it does not restrict the customer base or brand.

**Examples:** Is there space on the court? Is the item still on the shelf? How long is the queue? Avoid claiming guaranteed accessibility, safety or inventory from an unverified observation.

**Initial operating area:** Pick a place/use case where willing observers and genuine requesters overlap. This is an experiment to measure reliability, not a limit on the market. Broad branding does not require claiming nationwide observer coverage. Expansion follows evidence that supply and repeat demand work.

**Current paid feature:** Plus unlocks additional local saved-place collections. It is useful, implemented, and straightforward to connect to RevenueCat. It does not demonstrate willingness to pay for live observations or establish the marketplace business model. Do not pitch collection purchases as proof that the core startup is validated.

**Core business questions:** How often does a person need an answer? Can someone nearby answer quickly enough? Why will they participate? How much does a useful answer cost to fulfill, including failed requests and moderation? Does the requester return? A strong animation cannot answer those questions.

**Competition path:** Prioritize a truthful, reproducible student entry. Consider Design only if the actual qualifying store build and required integration are available by the deadline. Adding unrelated sponsor SDKs just to chase more categories increases work and dilutes the product.

**Scope discipline:** Get the native purchase verified; publish the exact tested source; record an honest demo; submit complete materials. In parallel with later product development, validate the request/observer need using real people. Do not imply that the backend, traction or trust systems have been completed in this change.
